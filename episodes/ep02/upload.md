**Description:**
Bo & Bloop · Episode 2 · Count with Bo

The starfish are wiggling all over the sand, and Bo counts twelve! But there are only ten... Bloop the jellyfish shows Bo a smart trick: move each starfish as you count it, so you never count one twice. The last number you say tells you how many!

In this episode kids learn:
- Counting from 1 to 10
- Move it, then count it: never count the same thing twice
- The last number tells you how many
