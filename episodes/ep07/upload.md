Bo & Bloop · Episode 7 · Count with Bo

Bo's Bubble Rocket is ready to fly... but one bolt is missing! Bloop the jellyfish finds it in the sand. Then Bo tries "One, two, three, go!" and "Ten, nine, seven..." and the rocket won't go. Count down from ten to one with Bo & Bloop, blast off, and sing the Countdown Song!

In this episode kids learn:
- Counting backwards from 10 to 1
- Each number is one less than the one before
- Don't skip a number: after nine comes eight!
