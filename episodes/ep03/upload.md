**Description:**
Bo & Bloop · Episode 3 · Shape Reef

Bo wants to build a rock tower, but his round rocks keep rolling away! Bloop the jellyfish shows Bo the difference: circles are round with no corners, so they roll. Squares have four straight sides and four corners, so they stay put. Count the corners, sort the rocks and sing the Circle & Square Song!

In this episode kids learn:
- Circles are round and have no corners
- Squares have 4 straight sides and 4 corners
- Sorting things by shape
