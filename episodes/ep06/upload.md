Bo & Bloop · Episode 6 · Bubble Bay Songs

Choo-choo! Bo has a Week Train with seven carriages, one for every day of the week. But the days are all mixed up: Monday, Friday, Tuesday, Sunday... and the train won't go! Bloop the jellyfish helps Bo put the days in order. Guess along: what comes after Monday? Then sing the Days of the Week Song with Bo & Bloop!

In this episode kids learn:
- There are 7 days in a week
- The days in order: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
- After Sunday, a new week starts with Monday again
