Bo & Bloop · Episode 5 · Why, Bo?

Bo has a new Float-or-Sink tank! Bo thinks big things sink and small things float... but his little pebble sinks and his big beach ball floats! Bloop the jellyfish shows Bo how to test and see. Guess along with Bo: will the metal key float or sink? What about the rubber duck? Then watch the magic bottle trick and sing the Float or Sink Song!

In this episode kids learn:
- Light things, and things full of air, float
- Heavy, solid things sink
- Big or small does not tell us: guess, then test and see!
