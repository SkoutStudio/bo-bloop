Bo & Bloop · Episode 4 · Bloop's Big Feelings

Bo is doing his fish puzzle, but the last piece just won't fit! Bo gets grumpy, and a little grey cloud pops up over his head. Bloop the jellyfish shows Bo three things to do when you feel grumpy: name the feeling, take three slow breaths, and ask for help. Breathe along with Bo, help him find the grumpy face, and sing the Grumpy Feelings Song!

In this episode kids learn:
- Naming a feeling: "I feel grumpy"
- Taking 3 slow breaths to feel calm (smell the flower, blow the bubble)
- Asking a friend for help when something is tricky
